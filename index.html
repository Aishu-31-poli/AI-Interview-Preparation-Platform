Before Interview

  • Research the company.
  • Revise important concepts.
  • Prepare your introduction.

  • Listen carefully.
  • Speak clearly.
  • Think before answering.
  • Stay confident.

  • Explain your approach.
  • Use examples.
  • Understand fundamentals.
  • Practice coding regularly.

  • Practice again.
  • Improve communication.
